phi-redact Upload Guard

Scans files locally, in your browser, before you upload them, and offers a redacted copy when it finds PII or PHI. All inference runs on this device.

Scanning

Redaction style

Per-label thresholds Override the global threshold for specific labels (for example, NAME_PATIENT at 0.50, or a custom recognizer label). Labels not listed here use the global threshold. Rules-based detections (SSN, cards, ...) have no score and always redact.

Allowlist and denylist

One term per line, matched case-insensitively. Allowlisted terms are never redacted — a term protects exactly the value it names. Denylisted terms are always redacted (labeled CUSTOM), even if the model and rules miss them; a term on both lists is redacted.

Custom recognizers

Your own patterns, matched alongside the built-in detection. Each recognizer gives its matches a label and a score; the score is gated by the threshold like the model's. Context words boost the score when one appears near a match. A validator (checksum) can gate matches so only values that pass are flagged. Invalid recognizers are not saved.

    Privacy and limits

    File contents are read only inside your browser and passed to the extension's own offscreen document for scanning. The extension makes exactly two kinds of network requests: downloading the model files from Hugging Face, and downloading the English OCR language data from jsDelivr (once, then cached). Neither request contains anything about your files.

    phi-redact is a detection aid, not a certified de-identification tool. It makes no HIPAA Expert Determination or Safe Harbor claim. Review its detections before relying on a redacted copy.
